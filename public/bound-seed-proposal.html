What genuinely mitigates the position

  • The xBND income leg. $316,178 accrues in rwaUSD across the horizon — 13% of capital — independent of the BND price. Only the private rounds hold this right.
  • Entry discount. Seed enters 50.4% below launch, so the position absorbs a 50% fall from launch before reaching cost.
  • Fixed supply. 1,000,000,000 BND exists at genesis and cannot be increased. Two burn sinks reduce it; 1.63% is retired across the horizon.
  • Cliff structure. No Seed tokens are sellable through the launch window.

What does not

  • There is no capital protection. No portion of this investment is guaranteed, insured, escrowed or principal-protected. The full amount is at risk.
  • Launch liquidity is thin. The BND/USDC pool holds $1.00M at generation, about 2.0% of FDV. Constant-product depth converts a large sale into an amplified price move, and this single parameter drives most of the −57% drawdown above.
  • The model is revenue-dependent. Every buy-side engine traces to Protocol Reserve revenue, which is the junior 20% claim on a fee base that must scale broadly as projected.
  • Delivery risk. The protocol is mid-programme. Audit and public security competition are scheduled but not complete.
No capital protection is offered
The reference form for this proposal contained a protection mechanism apportioning capital between protected and at-risk. No such mechanism exists in the BOUND structure, and none is represented here. Any protection would have to be documented in the SAFT and is not.
